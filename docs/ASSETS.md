# ResolveOS image assets

## Faithful evidence

`assets/screenshots/` contains screenshots the project owner supplied from the running UI. `assets/product-tour.gif` and `assets/replay-showcase.gif` animate the screenshots with transitions and pans; **these are illustrative montages, not captures of live interactions**. `hero-banner.png` is original graphic design composed from those supplied screenshots.

## Concept artwork

`assets/concept-art/` contains AI-stylized design concepts based on the screenshots. They are **not screenshots** and should not be used as proof of a live feature, endpoint, model, or metric. Some concept art includes illustrative local URLs and other placeholders; these are not deploy links.

## Privacy check

No API keys or passwords should appear in README, screenshots or assets. Always inspect new screenshots before publishing; mask account data, personal customer information, tokens, and addresses.
